VICE FRAME is an original multi-panel GTA-style cover maker that runs fully offline in the browser. Give it a photograph and it lays your pictures into the slanted poster format — leaning panels divided by dark gutters and white seams, with bold caption lettering across the lower third — then exports the finished cover as a PNG to share.

It offers three layouts — Triptych (3 panels), Quad (4) and Duo (2) — with a photo in every panel: click a panel on the cover to select it, drag to pan its photo, and set its zoom independently of the others. A single slider blends the colour grade from a warm sunset correction with an amber-rose wash into a saturated neon look with a magenta-cyan wash, and the caption follows the grade — a big uppercase title fitted to the cover's width with a smaller subtitle above it, typeset in the bundled Anton typeface with a heavy ink outline. Covers export at 1080×1080 (square), 1080×1350 (portrait) or 1920×1080 (wide), rendered at full resolution.

Everything here is original to this entry — the name, the design and the three bundled sample photographs (Pier, Boulevard, Beach) were made for it, and no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used. The app lives in `app/` and is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: no build step, no dependencies, nothing to install.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
